How the ranking works

Every protocol below records no token in the DefiLlama snapshot and is not a wrapped, staked or bridged product of a token that already trades. Its implied value is its TVL times its category’s market-cap-to-TVL multiple, taken from the protocols in that category whose tokens already trade (median, shrunk toward the global median, capped at 2.5×), adjusted for 7-day TVL growth (at most ±25%) and for how long it has run with no token (up to +20% at four years). The detail view shows the full working for each protocol. Nothing here predicts a launch; it prices one, if it happened, on the snapshot’s own figures.
